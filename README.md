## What this theory proposes

$$\Delta S = S_{BH} - S_{vN}, \qquad \eta = \frac{\Delta S}{S_{BH}} = 1 - \frac{S_{vN}}{S_{BH}}$$

- Entropic force law $F = \eta\, GMm/R^{2}$ — Newton recovered exactly at $\eta \to 1$

- Singularity avoidance ($\eta \to 0$ at Planckian saturation)
- Nucleon force hierarchy $\sim 10^{-38}$ reproduced
- Galaxy rotation-curve flatness, Bullet-Cluster gravity–baryon separation, vacuum-energy suppression (~120 orders), late-time self-acceleration — no dark particles, no cosmological constant
